# AI-Detected Image Classification
## Dataset Selection
Selected the GenImage dataset since:
- It neatly classifies our dataset into the three desired classes: GAN-generated, diffusion-generated and real images.
- Preprocessing is comparatively less heavy, and source leakage risk is also low(which occurs if sets of images come from different sources, so the model can learn the **source**(compression, resolution etc.), instead of the AI artifacts).

# For level 6
**Don't replace GenImage with MS-COCO-AI/Defactify as your main dataset.** As we established earlier, Defactify has **no GAN images** — it's diffusion/autoregressive generators only. Your whole Real/GAN/Diffusion framing (and the scientific justification for that 3-way split — GAN and diffusion leave structurally different artifacts) depends on GAN images existing in training. Swap it in as the *primary* set and you lose that framing entirely; you'd have to fall back to a binary or a subtler Real/Diffusion/Autoregressive split, which is strictly worse than what you already have.
**Do add it as a second, small OOD arm — this is a genuinely good idea and cheap.** At 150–300 images it's pure evaluation, no training, so it costs you almost nothing and it makes your audit's OOD story sharper. Here's why: your existing GLIDE hold-out tests **generator shift only** — GLIDE is a diffusion model your training never saw, but the real images are still ImageNet, same domain as training. Adding MS-COCO-AI as a second OOD set gives you **generator shift + domain shift simultaneously** (COCO real photos are visually different from ImageNet reals — different composition, more natural scenes; and if it includes generators like SD3/SDXL, those are also different from what you trained on). That's a strictly harder, more realistic worst-case, and "we tested two independent OOD axes, not just one" is a more sophisticated claim in your writeup than a single hold-out.
One thing to handle carefully: if MS-COCO-AI includes **DALL·E 3**, that model is a diffusion-autoregressive hybrid — the exact edge case flagged earlier. Don't quietly bucket it as "diffusion" in your OOD labels; either exclude DALL·E 3 images from this arm, or keep them but label the ambiguity explicitly in your log/README so you're not misrepresenting ground truth.
